📌 What LiftED Cohort 2 Is About:

LiftED Cohort 2 is a flagship Foundational Literacy & Numeracy (FLN) intervention encompassing 420 public schools across 6 active zones, focusing on Grade 3 Hindi and Mathematics. The program operates under a rigorous Difference-in-Differences (DiD) evaluation framework (Readiness Score: 7.8 / 10) to track value-added student learning gains against control schools.

🎯 Key Diagnostic Takeaways:

  • Hindi Decoding Bottleneck: Basic Swar/Vyanjan recognition reaches 68%, but Matra symbol decoding drops sharply to 32% mastery.
  • Math Place Value Bottleneck: Single-digit addition is strong (64%), but two-digit place value carry operations drop to 36% mastery.
  • Teacher Practice Adoption: Observed practice rate stands at 66.8% (+8.4% MoM) with improved lesson alignment.
